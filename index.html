FAQ

Answers before the first call.

How early should we book Aurelia?

For weddings and destination events, 9 to 18 months is ideal. Corporate and brand events can often move faster depending on scope, venue, and vendor availability.

Do you work outside New York?

Yes. We produce destination weddings, retreats, launches, and private celebrations across the United States and internationally.

Do you have a minimum budget?

Most full-service events begin at $50k in total event spend. Smaller celebrations may be a fit through design or coordination support.

Can you work with vendors we have already chosen?

Absolutely. We can integrate existing partners, identify missing roles, and make sure every supplier is working from one clear production plan.
